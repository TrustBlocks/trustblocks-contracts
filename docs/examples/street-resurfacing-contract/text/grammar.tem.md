**Sec. 1. Background and Purpose.**

This contract consists of the selected Contractor performing street preservation and other associated work on designated streets in the Town of China Grove. All work and materials shall meet and be in accordance with the provisions of the Occupational Safety and Health Administration, the North Carolina Department of Labor, the North Carolina Department of Transportation Standard Specifications for Roads and Structures (latest edition), the NCDOT Roadway Standard Drawings (latest edition), and the Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (latest edition), together with the NC Supplement thereto. The Contractor shall keep itself fully informed of all federal, State, and local laws, ordinances, and regulations applicable to the work.

**Sec. 2. Services and Scope to be Performed.**

The work under this contract includes, but is not limited to, the selected Contractor performing crack and joint sealing, patching, leveling, milling, resurfacing, re-striping, re-marking, re-installation of permanent raised pavement markings, and incidental concrete work on designated streets in the Town of China Grove as identified by the Public Works Department, including mobilizations and demobilizations; all labor including special subcontracting; permits, licenses, and certifications; handwork, fabrications, tools, equipment, all materials, miscellaneous hardware, consumables, preparations, and adhesives; excavation and grading; hauling; traffic control and work zone safety operations; site security and security of the work; infrastructure removals and resettings; incidental infrastructure relocations; removal of debris, cleanup, and disposal; seeding and mulching; testing; touch-up repairs; and all else required as necessary in accordance with the Contract documents to the satisfaction of the Director and the Town of China Grove, at the charges set forth in Exhibit “G.”

Additional exhibits may be used to further define this Agreement when the Contractor and Town so agree. Any additional exhibits shall be designated with capitalized, sequential letters of the alphabet, shall be attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference as if fully recited, and shall become terms of this Agreement upon execution by both parties.

In this Contract, “services” means the services the Contractor is required to perform pursuant to this Contract and all of the Contractor's duties to the Town that arise out of this Contract. **Sec. 4a. Retainage.**

Retainage shall be governed by N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143-134.1. The Town shall withhold no retainage on contracts having a total project cost of less than one hundred thousand dollars (\$100,000). The Town may withhold retainage on contracts having a total project cost between \$100,000 and \$299,999. The Town shall withhold retainage on contracts whose total project cost exceeds \$300,000. When withheld, retainage shall not exceed five percent (5%) of each periodic payment. After the project is fifty percent (50%) complete, the Town shall withhold no further retainage from subsequent payments, provided that (i) the surety concurs in writing, (ii) the Contractor continues to perform satisfactorily, and (iii) any nonconforming work identified in writing by the engineer or the Town has been corrected by the Contractor and accepted. If the Town determines that the Contractor's performance is unsatisfactory, the Town may reinstate retainage of up to five percent (5%) from each subsequent payment. The Town may withhold amounts in excess of five percent (5%) for unsatisfactory job progress, defective construction not remedied, disputed work, third-party claims filed against the Town, or reasonable evidence that a third-party claim will be filed.

17.5 The following disputes are not subject to mediation: (i) a dispute seeking a non-monetary recovery; and (ii) a dispute seeking a monetary recovery of \$15,000 or less.

17.6 A dispute seeking the extension of any time limit set forth in an agreement to construct the Project shall be subject to mediation only if the damages that would be suffered by the party seeking the extension would exceed \$15,000 if the disputed extension is denied. To the extent liquidated damages are set forth as the measurement of damages for failure to meet such time limit, those liquidated damages shall be the exclusive standard for determining the amount of damages associated with such dispute.

17.7 A dispute is limited to the recovery of monetary damages from the same transaction or occurrence against a single party or two or more parties alleged to be liable jointly, severally, or in the alternative. 17.8 In addition to such matters as are required by the Rules, a request for mediation shall include the amount of the monetary relief requested.

17.9 Prior to requesting mediation, a party must form a good faith belief, based on a reasonable and prudent investigation, that it is entitled under applicable law to recover the monetary amount to be included in the request from one or more of the remaining parties.

17.10 If a party breaches Section 17.9, it shall indemnify and hold harmless all other parties from any costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees and other costs of litigation, and damages incurred by such other parties arising from the breach.

**Crack and Joint Sealing Section Bid**

This section of the bid is for crack sealing longitudinal and transverse cracks and joints with PS/AR (hot-poured rubber asphalt) on streets designated for such work in the Special Conditions. It is the intent of this contract that crack sealing operations be accomplished in an expeditious manner during cooler weather conditions (between the months of September and December). The Contractor will not be required to seal existing edge joints. Scheduling shall be coordinated with the Town.

Equipment. A hot compressed air (HCA) lance shall be used to blast out vegetation, dirt, dampness, and loose material from the cracks and joints. A trailer-mounted 190-gallon safety-tested crack sealant preheater melter kettle, with horizontally mounted full-sweep double paddle agitator, shall be used to preheat the sealant.

Construction Requirements. All construction methods shall be in accordance with Section 657-3 of the Standard Specifications; follow the manufacturer's recommendations. Sealing operations shall not be conducted when the surface temperature of the pavement is below 32°F. Excessive overbanding or waste of material will not be tolerated. Immediately squeegee the crack or joint to minimize the height of the overband. All cracks or joints shall have a minimum 1/8-inch depth of sealant installed. After sealing, promptly remove any excess sealer from the pavement. Traffic is not to be permitted over the sealed areas unless approved by the Director. Sand or the manufacturer's recommended material may be applied on top of the sealant to prevent traffic pick-up.

Method of Measurement and Basis of Payment. The quantity of sealing existing pavement cracks and joints will be measured by the actual number of pounds of PS/AR material satisfactorily used to seal pavement cracks and joints on the designated streets. Any material spilled, used in excessive overbanding, wasted, misapplied, or unsatisfactorily used will be deducted in determining quantities for payment, as determined by the Director. **SC-1. Questions by Bidders to the Town**

All questions about the meaning or intent of the bidding documents and the contract documents shall be submitted in writing to the Director. Questions received less than ten (10) days prior to the date for opening of bids may not be answered. Only answers issued by Addendum will be binding. Oral interpretations or clarifications are without legal effect.

**SC-2. Project Location**

Locations of Town-maintained streets and other specified areas under the contract work proposed for street preservation operations are as indicated on Attachment 1 — Street Schedule, or as designated in writing by change order by the Director. Attachment 1 and any associated communication are made part of these Special Provisions and Conditions. The Director may, in an emergency situation, call for work by the Contractor. Streets not on the Town's municipal street system, and State-maintained routes within the Town's corporate limits, are excluded from this contract unless expressly listed on Attachment 1.

**SC-3. Bid and Bid Bond**

A bid may be modified or withdrawn by the submitting Bidder at any time prior to the opening of bids. If, within twenty-four (24) hours after bids are opened, a Bidder files a duly signed written notice with the Town and promptly thereafter demonstrates to the reasonable satisfaction of the Town that there was a material and substantial mistake in the preparation of its bid, that Bidder may withdraw its bid and the bid security will be returned. Thereafter, that Bidder will be disqualified from further bidding on the work provided under the contract documents. A bond or deposit for the bid is required in the amount of five percent (5%) of the total bid amount, in accordance with N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143-129(b).

**SC-4. Award, Waiver of Informalities, and Right of Rejection**

The Town of China Grove reserves the right to waive informalities and to reject any or all bids. Award, if made, will be by the China Grove Town Council to the lowest responsible, responsive bidder, taking into consideration quality, performance, and the time specified for performance of the work, and is subject to the receipt of the minimum number of bids required by N.C. Gen. Stat. § 143-132 and to the appropriation of funds under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 159-28.

**SC-10. Certain Provisions Relating to Project Construction**

**Milling:**

- Milling shall be performed in such a manner as to control the dust generated and mitigate mill dust from the Contractor's operations.
- The Contractor is to provide areas outside the right-of-way to dispose of milled material, which becomes the property of the Contractor. Any cost associated with this is incidental to the pay items associated with this part of the work.
- All milled roads must have binder placed no later than three (3) calendar days after completion of milling.
- Milled surfaces left open to traffic shall be signed in accordance with the MUTCD, and any milled edge exceeding two inches adjacent to a travel lane shall be protected or wedged.

**Asphalt Repaving:**

- Pave each section of roadway begun in a continuous operation. Do not begin work on another section of roadway unless satisfactory progress is being made toward completion of intersections and all other required incidental work, except for milling and patching operations.
- Place asphalt concrete in trench sections with asphalt pavement spreaders made for the purpose, or with other equipment approved by the Director.
- The actual asphalt binder content will be established during construction by the Director within the limits established in the Standard Specifications. Certain listed streets could be rehabilitated and resurfaced using a mix type different from that specified in the contract pay items at the direction of the Director. In the event a different mix is specified by the Director or proposed in writing by the Contractor, and if the Contractor and the Director mutually agree to a contract unit price for the new mix type pay item, payment will be made in accordance with the appropriate method of measurement and basis of payment given in the General Conditions and by written change order.

**SC-11. Damages and Repairs**

- The Town of China Grove will not be responsible for any claim or damages brought by property owners arising out of the Contractor's operations. Should the Contractor fail to make necessary damage repairs within a reasonable period of time as determined by the Director to any public or private infrastructure damaged by the Contractor or a subcontractor within the course of the work, the Town will make repairs and shall be reimbursed by the Contractor for all labor, tools, and materials necessary to correct the failure. Such reimbursements shall be deducted from money due the Contractor or shall be billed to the Contractor.
- The Contractor will be responsible for protection, prevention of vandalism, and maintenance of asphalt work, permanent thermoplastic pavement lines and markings, and newly installed fresh-poured concrete such as curb and gutter, wheelchair ramps, and sidewalk sections, prior to acceptance by the Town.

**SC-12. Inspection and Final Acceptance of the Work**

The Director or the Director's designated representative reserves the right to perform all inspection and to make all final acceptance of the work. If failure of an item of work occurs within the time of the contract, the Contractor shall begin to make necessary repairs within two (2) weeks, weather permitting. Should the Contractor fail to make necessary repairs within the established time frames, the Town of China Grove will make repairs and shall be reimbursed by the Contractor for all labor, tools, and materials necessary to correct the failure. Such reimbursements shall be deducted from money due the Contractor or shall be billed to the Contractor.

**SC-13. Preconstruction Conference**

Prior to the start of work, the Contractor shall attend a preconstruction conference at China Grove Town Hall with the Director, the Town Manager or designee, and representatives of affected utilities and emergency services. The Contractor shall bring a proposed schedule of operations by street, a traffic control plan, a list of proposed subcontractors with license numbers, and contact information for its superintendent and after-hours emergency contact.

The Contractor shall place door hangers or equivalent written notice at each occupied address on a street not less than twenty-four (24) hours in advance of milling, paving, or striping operations on that street. The Town will provide the notice template. The cost of notification is incidental to the contract unit prices. The Contractor shall coordinate with the Town so that the Town may post notice on its website and social media channels.
