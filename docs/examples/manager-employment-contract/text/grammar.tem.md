The appointment of the Employee as {{positionTitle}} and Chief Administrator is hereby confirmed, and the Employer hereby agrees to employ the Employee upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

# SECTION 2. EMPLOYMENT

A. {{municipalityType}} hereby agrees to retain employment of said Employee as {{positionTitle}} of the {{municipalityType}} to perform the functions and duties specified in the {{municipalityType}} Charter, {{dutiesAuthority}}, and to perform such other legally permissible and proper duties and functions as the {{governingBodyName}} shall from time-to-time assign.

# SECTION 3. TERM

A. The term of Employee's employment shall be {{termYearsWritten}} ({{termYears}}) years, commencing on {{termStartDate}} and ending on {{termEndDate}}, unless earlier terminated pursuant to the provisions of this Agreement. This Agreement shall not renew automatically; any renewal or extension of the term beyond {{termEndDate}} shall require affirmative action of the {{governingBodyName}} recorded in its minutes. Either party may give notice of an intent not to renew at least {{nonRenewalNoticeMonthsWritten}} ({{nonRenewalNoticeMonths}}) months before the expiration date. Employee serves at the pleasure of the {{governingBodyName}}, and nothing herein shall be taken to prevent, limit or otherwise interfere with the right of the {{governingBodyName}} to terminate the services of Employee at any time, subject to the conditions in SECTION 6.

B. In the event the {{governingBodyName}} determines the services of the Employee are no longer needed or desired, the {{governingBodyName}} will comply with designated voting procedures and procedures contained in the {{municipalityType}} Personnel Policy to determine Employee's continued employment.

C. Employee agrees to remain in the exclusive employ of the {{municipalityType}} during the term of this Agreement. The term "employed", however, shall not be construed to include occasional teaching, writing, speaking or consulting performed on the Employee's time off, even if outside compensation is provided for such services. Such activities are expressly allowed, provided that in no case is any activity allowed which would present a conflict of interest with the {{municipalityType}}. In any event, Employee shall not spend more than {{outsideActivityHoursWritten}} ({{outsideActivityHours}}) hours per week teaching, writing, speaking, consulting or on other non-{{municipalityType}} related business without the prior approval of the {{governingBodyName}}.

# SECTION 4. COMPENSATION

A. The {{municipalityType}} agrees to pay Employee for {{pronounPossessive}} services rendered pursuant hereto an annual base salary of {{annualBaseSalary}} ({{annualBaseSalaryWritten}}) dollars, payable in installments at the same time as other employees of the {{municipalityType}} are paid.

C. The {{municipalityType}} agrees not to reduce Employee's salary and benefits during the term of {{pronounPossessive}} employment except to the degree of such a reduction across-the-board for all employees of the Employer.

# SECTION 5. OTHER EMPLOYMENT BENEFITS

A. Employee shall receive vacation, sick leave, compensatory time, travel allowance, disability insurance, health and life insurance, {{retirementPlanName}} benefits, and participation in an approved retirement plan upon such terms and conditions established under the Employer's personnel policy or other policy approved or established for employees of the {{municipalityType}}.

B. It is understood and agreed that Employee will be credited with {{pronounPossessive}} existing accrued vacation and sick leave upon execution of this Agreement.

C. Employee is currently vested under the {{retirementSystemName}}. Employer agrees that such retirement will remain intact and Employer will continue to fund Employee's retirement on the same basis as any full-time employee employed by Employer. By accepting this position, Employee will not lose any vesting, tenure, longevity, or years of service and will remain in the {{municipalityType}}'s {{retirementSystemName}}.

# SECTION 6. TERMINATION AND SEVERANCE

A. Employee may be terminated "for cause" as that term is defined in subpart G of this Section.

B. Subject to subparts C, D, E, and F of this Section, should Employee be terminated or discharged by the {{governingBodyName}} without cause and at such time as the Employee is willing and able to perform {{pronounPossessive}} duties under this Agreement, then, and in that event, the Employer agrees to:

(1) continue to pay the Employee {{pronounPossessive}} then existing base salary for {{severanceYearsWritten}} ({{severanceYears}}) years following the date of Employee's termination;

(2) continue to fund Employee's {{retirementPlanName}} plan for {{severanceYearsWritten}} ({{severanceYears}}) years following the date of Employee's termination in the same amount and percentage as Employee was receiving while employed;

(3) pay Employee for the value of any accrued, unused vacation time; and

(4) continue to enroll Employee in the {{municipalityType}}'s medical, dental, and vision insurance plans for {{severanceYearsWritten}} ({{severanceYears}}) years from the date of Employee's termination in the same manner they did while Employee was employed by the {{municipalityType}}.

C. In the event the Employee is terminated or discharged as set out in subpart B of this Section and then accepts and commences employment with any other employer at the same or higher salary than {{pronounPossessive}} existing salary with the {{municipalityType}} of {{municipalityName}}, then subpart B of this Section shall not apply and all payments under such provision shall cease.

D. In the event that Employee is terminated or discharged as set out in subpart B of this Section and then accepts and commences employment with another employer at a lower salary than what the Employee is currently earning, then the Employer shall pay to the Employee the difference between {{pronounPossessive}} existing salary with the {{municipalityType}} of {{municipalityName}} and the base salary of {{pronounPossessive}} new employer for the remaining portion of the period as set out in subpart B of this Section. Any other benefits as set out in this Agreement or as provided by {{municipalityType}} shall not be payable by the Employer to the Employee once Employee accepts and commences employment with any other employer.

E. In the event that Employee is terminated or discharged as set out in subpart B of this Section and begins receiving retirement benefits from any other federal, state, or local government employer where the total of such other federal, state, or local retirement benefits are equal to or greater than Employee's then-existing salary at the time of such termination or discharge, then Employee shall not receive further compensation or benefits from Employer as contemplated under this Section.

F. In the event that Employee is (a) either terminated or discharged as set out in subpart B of this Section or resigns with the mutual agreement of Employer and (b) has {{serviceCreditWindowMonthsWritten}} ({{serviceCreditWindowMonths}}) months or less remaining in order for {{pronounObject}} to retire with {{retirementServiceYearsWritten}} ({{retirementServiceYears}}) years of service credits under the {{retirementSystemName}}, then and in that event, Employer may purchase such service credits that will allow Employee to retire with {{retirementServiceYearsWritten}} ({{retirementServiceYears}}) years of service credit in such Retirement System. In such event, Employee will be treated as if {{pronounSubject}} has retired from the {{municipalityType}} of {{municipalityName}} and will be entitled to the same benefits as a similarly situated employee.

G. As used in this Section, "Termination for Cause" means termination by the Employer if Employee is terminated (a) because of {{pronounPossessive}} conviction of a felony or a misdemeanor (other than a minor traffic violation) involving assault, theft, alcohol, controlled substances, or moral turpitude; or (b) repeatedly fails or refuses, after written notice from Employer signed by a majority of board members with all members having knowledge of the notice, in a material respect to follow reasonable policies or directives established by Employer; or (c) willfully and persistently fails, after written notice from the Employer signed by a majority of board members with all members having knowledge of the notice, to attend to material duties or obligations imposed upon {{pronounObject}} under this Agreement; or (d) engages in a material conflict of interest with {{pronounPossessive}} obligation to the {{municipalityType}}; or (e) violates any provision of the personnel policy applicable to {{municipalityType}} employees if such violation would be grounds for termination of an employee under the same or similar conditions or circumstances.

H. If Employee becomes disabled, incapacitated or infirm, Employer shall make reasonable accommodations to allow Employee to continue employment. Upon receiving a written statement that Employee is incapable of performing {{pronounPossessive}} duties, the Employer may terminate Employee, with Employee receiving all accrued sick and vacation pay.

# SECTION 7. SCHEDULE

It is understood and agreed that Employee is an exempt employee for purposes of the Fair Labor Standards Act. It is also understood that as {{positionTitle}}, Employee may adjust {{pronounPossessive}} schedule to facilitate the requirements of {{pronounPossessive}} position.

# SECTION 8. ETHICAL STANDARDS

The Employer expects the Employee to adhere to the highest professional standards. The Employee's actions will always comply with those standards. The Employee agrees to follow the Code of Ethics of the {{ethicsCodeOrganization}} and the ethics rules, regulations, and laws of the State of {{stateName}}. Consistent with the standards outlined in the Code, the Employee shall not endorse candidates, make financial contributions, sign or circulate petitions, or participate in fundraising activities for individuals seeking or holding elected office, nor seek or accept any personal enrichment or profit derived from confidential information or misuse of public time.

Employer shall support Employee in keeping these commitments by refraining from any order, direction or request that would require Employee to violate the {{ethicsCodeOrganization}} Code of Ethics. Neither the governing body nor any individual member thereof shall request Employee to endorse any candidate, make any financial contribution, sign or circulate any petition, or participate in any fundraising activity for individuals seeking or holding elected office, nor to handle any matter of personnel on a basis other than fairness, impartiality, and merit.

# SECTION 9. PERFORMANCE EVALUATION

A. Employee shall receive an annual evaluation at which time the {{governingBodyName}} will provide feedback to Employee regarding improving performance for the upcoming year. The {{governingBodyName}} and Employee shall define such goals and objectives determined necessary for the proper operation of the {{municipalityType}} and in the attainment of the {{governingBodyName}} policy objectives. The {{municipalityType}} and Employee shall further establish a relative priority among those various goals and objectives, said goals and objectives to be reduced to writing.

# SECTION 10. DUES AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

# SECTION 11. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

B. The {{governingBodyName}} will vote to approve to budget and to pay for the travel and subsistence expenses of Employee for short courses, institutes, and seminars that are necessary for {{pronounPossessive}} professional development and for the good of the {{municipalityType}}.
